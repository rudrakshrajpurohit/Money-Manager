💰 Money Manager
----------------

A simple, calendar-aware budgeting web app that helps you follow the **50-30-20 rule** and calculate how much you can safely spend each day based on how much time is left in the month.

Instead of assuming every month has 30 days, this app looks at the real calendar and adjusts your daily spending limit dynamically.

✨ Features
----------

*   Enter your **monthly income (allowance)**
    
*   Automatically saves **20%** based on the 50-30-20 rule
    
*   Calculates how many days remain until the **1st of the next month**
    
*   Distributes your spendable money across the remaining days
    
*   Shows a **realistic daily spending limit**
    
*   Works in real time using JavaScript’s date system

📌 Why This Exists
------------------

Most budgeting apps assume static months and fixed daily budgets.Real life doesn’t work that way.

This app models **money as a flow through time**, which makes it far more realistic for students, freelancers, and anyone living month-to-month.
